### Lab 3. Toy shell

> touch toy_shell.py
> nano toy_shell.py
> python3 toy_shell.py
> Toy Shell: List files and folders older than a given date (based on creation time)
> Enter a date in the format YYYY-MM-DD or type 'exit' to quit.
> toy-shell> 2005-30-10
> Invalid date format. Please use YYYY-MM-DD.
> toy-shell> 2005-10-30
> toy-shell> 2026-09-15
> toy-shell> exit
> Exiting toy shell.
